TrioTrail Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

TrioTrail: Tile Match Puzzle ("the game") is made by Parbat Studio (Pankaj Parbat). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. If you have a question, write to pankajparbat6688@gmail.com.

No account, no personal details

You don't need an account to play. The game never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal details.

Your progress stays on your phone

Levels, stars, coins, boosters, collection items and settings are saved on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

Optional cloud backup

If you choose to create a backup code in Settings, a copy of your game progress is stored with Unity Cloud Save (a service of Unity Technologies) under an anonymous player created for that code. The code is the only way to restore it; it isn't linked to your name or email. If you never create a code, nothing is backed up.

Live game settings and analytics (Unity Gaming Services)

To run events and seasonal festivals without an app update, the game downloads its settings from Unity Remote Config. For this, Unity Authentication gives the game a random, anonymous player ID that is stored on your device.

The game also sends anonymous gameplay events to Unity Analytics, such as levels started and finished, boosters used, tutorials completed and rewards claimed, to help us improve the game and balance its difficulty. Where the law requires your consent (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), analytics only start after you agree in the consent form.

Unity receives the anonymous player ID together with basic technical information such as the app version, device model, operating system, approximate region derived from the IP address, and the events above. Unity processes this data on our behalf under its privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the menus, an occasional full-screen ad between levels, and optional reward videos you choose to watch. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about your device and how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Where the law requires it, a consent form from Google lets you choose whether ads may be personalised; you can change that choice at any time from Settings > Privacy Choices, and Settings also lets you turn personalised ads off. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. See how Google uses information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google Play tells the game only which item was bought, so it can be delivered and restored.

Children

The game is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Sharing

We don't sell your information. The services named above (Unity and Google) process data only to provide the features described here.

Security and retention

All network requests use encrypted connections (HTTPS). Anonymous analytics and backup data are kept only as long as needed for the purposes above.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Change your ad consent in Settings > Privacy Choices, or turn off personalised ads in Settings.
  • Uninstall the game or clear its data to remove everything stored on your device, including the anonymous ID.
  • To ask us to delete data held by Unity for your anonymous player ID or backup code, email pankajparbat6688@gmail.com.

How to delete your TrioTrail data

TrioTrail has no sign-up. The only account-like record is the anonymous Unity player created when you make a backup code. To delete it and everything linked to it:

  1. Email pankajparbat6688@gmail.com with the subject Delete my TrioTrail data.
  2. If you made a backup code, include it (Settings > Back Up Progress shows it). Without a code, tell us roughly when you installed the game and we will delete what we can identify.
  3. We delete the data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Deleted: your cloud backup of game progress, the anonymous Unity player ID linked to it, and the analytics events recorded under that ID.
Kept: nothing that identifies you. Purchase records are kept by Google Play under its own policies, and statistics already combined into totals contain no ID.
On your phone: uninstalling TrioTrail or clearing its data in Android settings deletes your local progress and the anonymous ID immediately.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
